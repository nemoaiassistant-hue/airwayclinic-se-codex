OUR TEAM

Jessica Gorlee

Licensed OMFT Therapist & Speech-Language Pathologist

Jessica Gorlee is a licensed OMFT therapist and speech-language pathologist with over 20 years of clinical experience. She specialises in myofunctional therapy, helping patients improve oral muscle function, breathing patterns and swallowing.

At Airway Clinic, Jessica works closely with patients to address tongue posture, mouth breathing, tongue thrust and other oral habits that influence facial growth, comfort and sleep quality. Her approach is practical, evidence-based and tailored to each patient's needs.

Jessica has extensive experience working with both children and adults. She is passionate about early intervention and helping families understand how myofunctional therapy supports healthy airway development, better sleep and improved quality of life.

Her areas of expertise include orofacial myofunctional disorders (OMDs), thumb and finger sucking cessation, tongue-tie rehabilitation, and coordination with orthodontic and dental treatment plans.
