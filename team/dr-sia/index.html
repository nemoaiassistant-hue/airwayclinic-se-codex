OUR TEAM

Dr. Siavosh Hakimmaani

D.D.S. — Founder & Lead Clinician

Dr. Siavosh Hakimmaani (Dr. Sia) is a Stockholm-based dentist with over 30 years of clinical experience specialising in airway-focused dentistry, orthodontics and craniofacial development.

He founded Airway Clinic to address the root causes of sleep-disordered breathing, jaw dysfunction and facial growth issues in both children and adults. His approach looks beyond symptoms to understand how oral function, breathing patterns and jaw balance affect long-term health.

Dr. Sia's practice integrates traditional dentistry with airway assessment, myofunctional therapy coordination, and early intervention strategies for children. He works closely with families to guide craniofacial development during critical growth periods.

His clinical interests include ALF therapy, orthodontic airway screening, TMJ treatment, and growth guidance programmes for children. He is passionate about educating both patients and fellow practitioners on the connection between oral health and whole-body wellness.
