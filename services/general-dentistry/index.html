What Is It

General Dentistry

General dentistry covers the essential care that keeps teeth, gums, and restorations healthy: examinations, cleanings, fillings, crowns, repair work, and long-term prevention. At Airway Clinic Stockholm, that routine care is delivered with a wider view of how oral health connects to breathing, sleep, jaw function, and overall wellbeing.

A checkup is not only a search for cavities. We also look for signs of tooth wear, clenching, gum inflammation, narrow arches, tongue posture, dry mouth, mouth breathing, and other patterns that can affect comfort and stability over time.

The goal is dentistry that is thorough, calm, and practical. We restore what needs attention, prevent avoidable problems, and help you understand the bigger picture behind your dental health.

Who Is It For

Who Is It For

Adults, teens, and families who want careful preventive and restorative dental care, including checkups, professional cleanings, fillings, crowns, cracked tooth care, gum monitoring, and second opinions. It is especially useful for patients who want their dental care to consider airway health, sleep quality, jaw tension, tooth wear, and long-term stability.

How It Works

How It Works

Every visit is designed to combine precise dental care with a broader functional assessment.

Comprehensive Examination

We assess teeth, gums, restorations, bite, tooth wear, oral habits, jaw comfort, and airway-related signs.

Prevention And Cleaning

Professional cleaning, hygiene guidance, and risk-based prevention help reduce inflammation, decay, and future repair needs.

Restorative Care

When treatment is needed, fillings, crowns, and repairs are planned for strength, comfort, appearance, and bite stability.

Long-Term Maintenance

Recall intervals and follow-up are tailored to your risk level, goals, airway considerations, and overall oral health.
