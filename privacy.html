Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Airway Clinic Stockholm ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or contact our clinic.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect:

  • Contact form data — name, email address, phone number and your stated concerns when you submit a consultation request.
  • Usage data — anonymous information about how you interact with our website, collected via Google Analytics. This includes pages visited, time spent on site, browser type, device type and general location (country/city level only).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your consultation request and provide relevant clinic information.
  • Improve our website content and user experience based on anonymised usage patterns.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. Google Analytics uses cookies to analyse website traffic. The data generated is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States.

Google Analytics collects anonymised data including:

  • Pages viewed and navigation path
  • Time spent on each page
  • Device type, browser and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral source (how you found our website)

We have enabled IP anonymisation, so Google does not receive your full IP address. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies required for Google Analytics to function. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party marketing cookies.

The cookies used are:

  • _ga — distinguishes unique visitors (2 years)
  • _ga_* — maintains session state (2 years)

5. Data Storage and Security

Contact form submissions are processed securely and stored in compliance with applicable data protection laws. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject within the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing of your data for specific purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@airwayclinic.se.
